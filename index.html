Delete your Tethar account

Halo Apps Ltd (“Tethar”) lets you request deletion of your parent account and related child-app data associated with that account.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@tethar.com from the email address on your Tethar account.
  2. Use the subject line: Account deletion request.
  3. Include your account email and, if known, the child profile name(s) linked to the account.

What we delete

  • Your parent account login and profile information
  • Linked child device data we store (for example settings, usage records, and location history we
